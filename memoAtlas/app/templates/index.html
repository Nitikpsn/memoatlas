TREE STAGES

Every tree grows through these stages based on how often you revise it.

Seed Health 1-20

A new idea, just planted. Revise it to help it grow.

Sprout Health 21-40

Taking root. The idea is becoming clearer.

Young Health 41-70

Growing strong. Branches are forming.

Mature Health 71-90

A well-established tree with a full canopy.

Ancient Health 91-100

Peak growth. Deeply ingrained knowledge.

Fading 30+ days unrevised

Neglected. The tree is losing color. Revise soon.

Wilting 45+ days unrevised

Near death. Urgent revision needed.

Dead 60+ days unrevised

The tree has died. It remains as a stump.

HOW IT WORKS

Each tree starts at 20 health. Revise it to add +20 health (max 100). If you skip 30 days, health decays 1 per day. Reach 0 health or 60 days unrevised and the tree dies.

Every revision earns XP and raises your level. A healthy forest means a healthy mind.
