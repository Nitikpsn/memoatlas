MEMOATLAS

A forest where your knowledge grows.

WHAT IS THIS?

MemoAtlas turns your notes into trees. Every idea you save starts as a seed. When you revise it, the tree grows. Neglect it, and it withers. The healthier your forest, the stronger your memory.

It is not a doc editor. It is a living atlas of your mind.

TREE STAGES

Every tree passes through these stages based on its health and how recently you revised it.

Seed Health 1-20

A new idea, just planted. Fragile but full of potential. Revise it to help it break ground.

Sprout Health 21-40

Taking root. The idea is becoming clearer. Keep revising to push it higher.

Young Health 41-70

Growing strong. The concept has structure. Branches are forming. Regular revision keeps it thriving.

Mature Health 71-90

A well-established tree. The idea is solid and deeply rooted. Full canopy of knowledge.

Ancient Health 91-100

Peak growth. This knowledge is deeply ingrained. A wise, towering tree in your forest.

Fading 30+ days unrevised

The first signs of neglect. The tree is losing its color. Revise soon or it will weaken further.

Wilting 45+ days unrevised

Near death. The tree is barely holding on. Urgent revision needed to save it.

Dead 60+ days unrevised

The tree has died. It remains in your forest as a stump. Reviving is not possible — plant a new tree.

HOW HEALTH WORKS

Each tree starts with 20 health. Revising a tree (reading its content and confirming you remember it) adds +20 health, up to a maximum of 100.

If you do not revise a tree for 30 days, its health begins to decay by 1 point per day. A tree that reaches 0 health or goes 60+ days without revision dies.

The revision page includes a 5-minute timer. Use that time to read the content and reflect. When the timer ends, the revision counts.

HOW TO GAIN XP

Every time you revise a tree, you earn XP. The more trees you tend, the more XP you accumulate, and your level rises. Higher levels reflect a well-maintained, thriving forest of knowledge.

  • Revising a tree earns XP
  • XP contributes to your player level
  • A healthy forest = a higher Forest Health score
  • Check your level on the Profile page

CONNECTING TREES

On any tree's detail page, you can link it to other trees. This creates visible connections between related ideas. Open the MAP view to see your entire forest as a network of connected thoughts.

GAME MODE

The GAME page tests your recall. It shows you a random tree description and you must pick the correct tree from your forest. A good way to check what you actually remember.

TIPS

  • Revise trees before they turn yellow (before day 30) to prevent decay
  • Use tags to organize trees by topic
  • Revise regularly to grow Ancient trees — they are the pride of your forest
  • Use the Game page to test recall of your weakest trees

Every great forest started as a single seed.
