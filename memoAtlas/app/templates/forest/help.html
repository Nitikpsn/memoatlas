HOW TO USE MEMOATLAS

Your mind is a forest. Here's how to grow it.

1. PLANT A TREE

Every idea, thought, or snippet of knowledge starts as a seed. Click "PLANT TREE" to create a new tree. Give it a title and write down what you want to remember. Over time, your tree will grow as you tend to it.

2. TEND YOUR TREES

Trees need care. Every 30 days, your trees will start to wither. Click "TEND" on any tree to revise it — read what you wrote and confirm you still remember it. This boosts the tree's health and helps it grow to the next stage.

3. WATCH THEM GROW

Trees grow through stages: Seed → Sprout → Young → Mature → Ancient. The more you revise, the bigger and healthier your trees become. Neglect them and they will Fade, Wilt, and eventually Die.

4. CONNECT IDEAS

On a tree's detail page, you can link it to other trees. This creates connections between related ideas, just like branches in a forest. Use the "MAP" view to see your whole forest as a network of connected thoughts.

5. EARN XP & LEVEL UP

Tending your trees earns you XP. As you gain XP, your level increases. Keep your whole forest healthy to see your Forest Health score grow. A healthy forest is a healthy mind.

6. SEARCH YOUR FOREST

Use the "SEARCH" button to quickly find any tree by title or content. No more lost thoughts — everything is just a search away.

TIPS

  • Tend trees before they turn yellow to keep them healthy
  • Use tags on your trees to organize by topic
  • Revise regularly to build a forest of ancient, wise trees
  • The game page tests your recall — pick the right tree for each description

Remember: every great forest started as a single seed.
